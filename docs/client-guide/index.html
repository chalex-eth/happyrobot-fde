05 / When a call needs attention

A clear outcome.
A clear next action.

Check the saved outcome before acting. Not every call ends in a booking or needs a manager decision.

What happenedWhat you seeWhat to do
01 / VERIFICATIONCarrier cannot
be verified		A verification outcome in All activity.Read the reason. Verification must succeed before load matching; it cannot be skipped.
02 / NO AGREEMENTNo suitable load
or rate agreement		A no-match, declined or no-agreement outcome.No booking to approve. Review only if there is an outstanding request.
03 / HUMAN HELPCaller wants help
or a callback		A follow-up item with the saved request and available contact details.Arrange contact outside the workspace. A recorded request does not place a call or send a message.
04 / BOOKING STATUSBooking is uncertain
or unsuccessful		Check submission status, or Submission failed.Check whether a booking exists before retrying. Verify availability and agree the next step with the carrier.
05 / INTERRUPTIONCall ends with
an unresolved issue		Resolve interrupted request, or Check unresolved request.Read the saved details, establish what remains to be done, and record the next step.
Read the request→Resolve the issue→Save a note

Complete the follow-up review after the work is done. A note is required. You can reopen a review with a note if more work is needed; closing it does not change a booking or contact the carrier.

HappyRobot LogisticsYour daily routine

06 / Keep the desk moving

Start with attention.
Finish with clarity.

A simple routine for every operator using the workspace.

  1. 01Open Needs attention.

    Review approvals and outstanding requests. Check pickup urgency and waiting time.

  2. 02Read the full request.

    Check carrier, load, rate and the reason for review before deciding.

  3. 03Act, then leave a note.

    Approve, request changes, reject or arrange follow-up. Record what was resolved.

  4. 04Review the period.

    Check requests, confirmations and negotiation acceptance. Keep demo activity in context.

Know your demo workspace.

Connected & recorded

Carrier authority checks and live load inventory.

Voice conversations, saved outcomes, negotiation details and review decisions.

Simulated & manual

Codes appear on screen. Booking approval and references are simulated.

Your team handles carrier contact and documents. No live transfer, automatic callback or notification is sent.

If you need help

Share the load ID or carrier MC, the visible status and what happened with your project contact. Keep passwords and verification codes private.
